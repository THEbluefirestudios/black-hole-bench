TRACING LIGHT THROUGH SPACETIME

Inside the observatory

Light takes the long way.

Each pixel follows a light ray through the curved spacetime of a spherical, non-rotating black hole. Some rays escape to the stars. Others cross the glowing disc, orbit the hole, or disappear past the event horizon.

The arc above the shadow is the far side of the same disc, bent into view by gravity. The narrower images below and around the shadow come from light taking a longer route.

The disc begins at the innermost stable circular orbit, 6 GM/c². Orbital motion brightens the approaching side; gravitational redshift changes the light’s colour. Drag to orbit and see how the geometry changes.
