Behind the light
One disc. A curved universe.
The arch above the shadow is the far side of the same flat accretion disc. Light bends around the black hole, bringing normally hidden regions into view. Orbit the camera to see the geometry unfold.
The model
Light paths are integrated through a Schwarzschild (non-spinning) spacetime. The horizon is at 1 rₛ, the photon sphere at 1.5 rₛ, and the thin disc extends from the innermost stable circular orbit at 3 rₛ to 13 rₛ. A static observer sits at 32 rₛ.
Gravitational redshift and orbital Doppler beaming modulate the disc. Its filaments, turbulence, amber palette, glow, and animation time scale are artistic choices inspired by Interstellar. This is an interactive visualization, not a fluid simulation or the film’s rotating Kerr black hole.
Switch lensing off to compare against straight light paths. The star field is procedural, not an astronomical catalogue.
